What stands out for the Education Director

  • New enrolments kept arriving through week three — 56 net new entrants have pushed the roll 21.9% above last session, to 178.
  • Boarding is up to 57 (+3 since the mid-census count), with boys back to 30, though still below last session’s 35; girls are unchanged at 27.
  • SS3 has 31 students — nearly double the 16 who graduated — plan exams and valedictory for a larger Class of 2027.
  • Boys hold a clear majority in Year 8; girls are 73% of SS2.
  • Year 9 is the most gender-balanced level this term.
  • Withdrawals revised to 8 (5 itemised by class transition, 3 still to be confirmed) — worth reconciling against the admissions register.
